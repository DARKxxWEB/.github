## // WHO WE ARE

DARK@WEB is a security-focused team exploring the intersection of
cybersecurity, cryptography, and artificial intelligence.

We focus on understanding complex systems, analyzing attack surfaces,
researching security weaknesses, and engineering intelligent solutions
with precision, discretion, and a research-driven mindset.

Our work is guided by one principle:

> **Trust Nothing. Verify Everything.**

## // CORE DOMAINS

- **Cybersecurity & Penetration Testing**
  Security assessment, attack-surface analysis, and authorized testing.

- **Cryptography & Cryptanalysis**
  Cipher analysis, cryptographic research, and algorithmic exploration.

- **AI & Intelligent Systems**
  Intelligent security applications, algorithmic experimentation,
  and AI-driven research.

- **Network & Application Security**
  Network defense, application assessment, and security architecture.

## // OUR PRINCIPLES

- **Precision** — Every detail matters.
- **Research** — Understand the system before challenging it.
- **Confidentiality** — Respect sensitive information and privacy.
- **Zero Trust** — Verify explicitly. Assume nothing.
- **Responsible Security** — Test and research within authorized boundaries.

## // PROJECTS

Our repositories may include selected research, experimental implementations,
security utilities, and technical explorations.

Some projects and internal research remain private.

Only publicly available repositories represent work intended for
external access and review.

## // OPERATIONAL ETHOS

We value technical depth over noise, evidence over assumptions,
and engineering over empty claims.

No unnecessary exposure.
No blind trust.
No compromise on precision.
